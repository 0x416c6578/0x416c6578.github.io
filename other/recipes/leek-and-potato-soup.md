## Leek and Potato Soup
### Ingredients
- 4 large leeks
- 2 medium potatoes, not waxy ones
- 1 white onion
- 1.5 pints chicken / veg stock
- 0.5 pints milk
- 50g butter
- (Optional) garlic

### Instructions
1. Cut up leeks, wash
2. Chop up onion, potato into cubes
3. Add butter, leeks, onion (+ garlic) and potato to big pot, cook on low until all softened
4. Add stock and milk, simmer for >20 mins
5. Blend
