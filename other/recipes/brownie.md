## Brownie
### Ingredients / Implements
- 20cm brownie tin
- 200g dark chocolate
- 100g milk chocolate
- 250g butter
- 300g caster sugar
- 3 large eggs
- 60g self raising flour
- 60g cocoa powder

### Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 160 fan, grease tin with butter and line with baking paper
2. Snap dark chocolate into bowl, add butter and salt
3. Microwave in 30 second bursts, mix until melted
4. Chop milk chocolate roughly
5. Measure sugar into bowl, crack in eggs and whisk until they are light and fluffy and 2x in size
6. Pour in melted choc and butter, whisk briefly until combined
7. Put half the batter in the tin, sprinkle in 1/2 milk choc, add rest of batter, add rest of choc, add salt on top
8. Bake for 30 mins or until edges set and middle is still wobbling
